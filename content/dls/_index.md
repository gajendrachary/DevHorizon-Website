### Brand Color Tokens
| Token | Hex Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Background** | `#111315` | Canvas & dark viewports |
| **Cards & Surfaces** | `#24272B` | Containers, card panels, dialogs |
| **Text & Light Details** | `#D4D8DE` | Primary legible copy & headings |
| **Shining Silver** | `#E8EAED` | Metallic Primary Accent |
| **Secondary Platinum** | `#C0C5CC` | Secondary Accent, subtitles, borders |
| **White Sections** | `#FFFFFF` | Architectural contrast services |

---

### Typography Stack
- **Display & Headings**: `Manrope` / `Inter` (Geometric sans-serif)
- **Monospace**: `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas`

---

### Component Overview
1. **Buttons (`.dls-btn`)**:
   - `.dls-btn-silver`: Primary brushed metallic silver pill CTA
   - `.dls-btn-secondary`: Obsidian dark container with silver border
   - `.dls-btn-inverted`: Dark button for pure white architectural sections
   - `.dls-btn-ghost`: Minimalist transparent action
2. **Badges (`.dls-badge`)**:
   - `.dls-badge-silver`, `.dls-badge-dark`, `.dls-badge-outline`, `.dls-badge-success`
   - Real-time pulse dot `.dls-dot`
3. **Surfaces (`.dls-card-dark`, `.dls-card-white`, `.dls-card-project`)**:
   - High-contrast bimodal card design for both obsidian backgrounds and architectural white zones.
